How it works ↓
Prototype · 4 scenarios · FY 2025-26 + every month · fully synthetic data

What we do

Three things, done honestly.
◈

Attribute the gap

Every MWh of the miss is assigned to a named cause — irradiance, temperature, downtime, curtailment, soiling, degradation, model bias — independent of the order causes are evaluated.
◎

Show what is unknown

The unexplained remainder gets its own bar, its own 90% band, and a verdict: within measurement noise, or a real signal asking for string-level logs and thermal scans.
✓

Prove it on truth

We generate the plant, so we know the true causes — including one deliberately hidden loss. Validation shows injected vs recovered numbers with no invented causes.

How we do it

Deterministic engine, no black boxes, no LLM arithmetic.
1

Simulate

An hourly PV model builds a P50 forecast year and the measured year from sensors, logs and a meter.
2

Attribute fairly

All 128 cause on/off coalitions are simulated; each cause gets its Shapley value — the average over all 5,040 orderings.
3

Bound uncertainty

80 Monte Carlo runs perturb sensors, meter and calibration and recalibrate every time, giving 90% bands.
4

Classify & validate

Causes are labelled predictable, controllable, luck or outside operator control, then checked against known truth.
